# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability. Participation
requires respect for other contributors, maintainers, operators, and users.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps the project include:

- discussing ideas and evidence respectfully;
- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- accepting correction and taking responsibility for mistakes;
- respecting privacy, licenses, attribution, and coordinated disclosure;
- following the radio-safety and clean-room rules in `AGENTS.md`;
- prioritizing the safety of operators and bystanders.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, insults, discrimination,
sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publishing private data,
or retaliation against someone who reports a concern. Unauthorized testing
against another person's account, deployment, station, radio, or transmitter is
also unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
project-managed spaces, and public interactions where someone represents the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other
conduct concerns, contact repository owner
[@KingSteve032](https://github.com/KingSteve032) through an available private
GitHub channel. Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code. Reports will
be reviewed as privately and impartially as practical. Retaliation against a
reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
